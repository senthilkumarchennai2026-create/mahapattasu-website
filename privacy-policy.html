Privacy Policy

How Sri Mahalakshmi Pyro (Maha Pattasu) collects, uses, and protects your information.

Home›Privacy Policy
Last updated: July 2025

1. Introduction

Sri Mahalakshmi Pyro, operating under the trade name Maha Pattasu ("we", "our", or "us"), is a GST-registered fireworks retail reseller based in Vembakottai, Virudhunagar District, Tamil Nadu. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you interact with our website mahapattasu.com.

This is an enquiry-generation website only. We do not process online payments, accept credit card details, or store order data on our servers. All transactions are completed through direct WhatsApp or telephone communication.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you contact us via WhatsApp or phone, you may share:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp number or phone number
  • Your delivery address or city
  • Your product requirements and order preferences

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

Our website may collect standard web analytics data such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Browser type and device type
  • Referring website or search engine
  • Geographic location (country/state level only)

This data is collected through cookies and analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) and does not personally identify you.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide through WhatsApp or phone is used solely to:

  • Respond to your product enquiry
  • Provide pricing, availability, and delivery information
  • Process and coordinate your order
  • Arrange delivery of your purchased products

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, nor do we share it with any third party outside of the delivery fulfilment process.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics – for understanding website traffic and user behaviour (anonymised)
  • Google Fonts – for serving website typography
  • WhatsApp – as the primary communication and enquiry channel (operated by Meta Platforms)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them if you have concerns.

5. Data Retention

Enquiry information shared via WhatsApp is retained in the WhatsApp conversation for the purpose of order fulfilment. We do not maintain a separate customer database or CRM system. Analytics data is retained as per Google Analytics default settings.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies for analytics purposes. By continuing to use this website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the website may not function correctly as a result.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request that we delete any personal information you have shared with us through WhatsApp or phone. To make such a request, please contact us at the number listed below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available at mahapattasu.com/privacy-policy.html. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of any updates.
