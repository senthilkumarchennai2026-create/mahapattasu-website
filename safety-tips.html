Essential Guidelines

8 Golden Safety Rules

Always read the instructions on every cracker before use. These eight rules can make the difference between a joyful celebration and an avoidable accident.

👨‍👩‍👧

Adult Supervision Always

Fireworks must only be lit by responsible adults. Keep children at a safe distance and never leave them unsupervised near crackers or sparklers at any time.

🌳

Open Outdoor Spaces Only

Always burst crackers in open outdoor areas — away from buildings, trees, vehicles, and any flammable materials. Never use them indoors or in confined spaces.

🪣

Keep Water & Sand Nearby

Always have a bucket of water or sand within arm's reach before you start. These are your first line of defence in any accidental fire or burn situation.

🚫

Never Relight a Dud

If a cracker fails to ignite, do not attempt to relight it. Wait at least 30 minutes, then soak it thoroughly in water before carefully disposing of it in a bin.

📦

Safe & Dry Storage

Store all crackers in a cool, dry place away from heat sources, naked flames, and direct sunlight. Never store fireworks inside the home near fuel, gas, or electrical equipment.

👕

Wear Cotton Clothing

Always wear full-sleeved, close-fitting cotton clothes when handling fireworks. Avoid synthetic fabrics, which can catch fire and melt instantly, causing severe burns.

🙅

Never Point at People or Property

Never aim or throw crackers at another person, animal, or property — even as a prank. Rockets and ground-spinners should always be directed into clear open sky or ground.

♻️

Safe Disposal After Cooling

Once celebrations are over, allow spent crackers to cool completely before handling. Soak used fireworks in water and dispose of them responsibly in a designated waste bin.
